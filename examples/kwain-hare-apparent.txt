# The Hare Apparent — Kwain, Itinerant Meddler (Azorius rabbits, bracket 3 or under).
#
# Twenty-two Hare Apparents, each one a courtier you can name on the blank
# of its own print. Kwain hands the table a card a turn so nobody minds the
# rabbits multiplying; blinks and bounces re-trigger the Hares for more.
#
# Balthier and Fran is {1}{R}{G} — Gruul — so it cannot sit in an Azorius
# colour identity. Left out rather than making the deck illegal.
#
# Theming picks, in list order:
#   Ambling Stormshell ....... the giant tortoise (mountain-sized shell, no house on it)
#   Bill Ferny, Bree Swindler  the "Fern" card, and a swindler besides
#   Claim Jumper ............. a Rabbit outlaw — no Rabbit Rogue exists in Magic
#   Daring Apprentice ........ pinned to 8ED for the flavour: "I've seen my master
#                              do this a hundred times. How hard can it be?"
#   Dramatic Accusation ...... the framing; Sakashima the Impostor is the man
#                              who walked away wearing someone else's face
#   First Response ........... a house burning, and a soldier running at it
#   Portal of Sanctuary ...... the magic portal
#   Syndicate Messenger ...... a crime syndicate operating in mono-white — the Plains
#   Vampire Slayer, Wooden Stake  no vampires here, but somebody is expecting them
#   Warren Elder ............. the father of the warren, a Rabbit Cleric

#   Printings are pinned where the automatic pick landed on a Secret Lair
#   (the text-only "YOU ARE EXILED" Path to Exile, and friends) — swapped for
#   painted fantasy versions: Lockwood, Ejsing, Giancola, Palumbo, rk post.
