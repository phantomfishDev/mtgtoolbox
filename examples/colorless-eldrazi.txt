# Colorless Eldrazi — Zhulodok, Void Gorger. Big spells that bring friends.
#
# Zhulodok reads: "Colorless spells you cast from your hand with mana value 7
# or greater have 'Cascade, cascade.'" One seven-drop is three spells. He costs
# {5}{C}, so he lands on turn four or five and every fatty after him pays for
# two more.
#
# Everything in the list follows from that one line.
#
# 1. The cascade only looks at MANA VALUE, never at what you paid. Eye of Ugin
#    and Conduit of Ruin knock {2} off an Eldrazi without lowering its mana
#    value, so a discounted Ulamog still cascades twice. Cost reduction is not
#    a compromise here, it is free value.
#
# 2. It must be cast FROM YOUR HAND. Cascade's own free casts do not chain, the
#    {20} on Spawnsire does not chain, and Anticausal Vestige recast from exile
#    does not chain. Nothing in here is built to cheat spells into play,
#    because cheating them in turns the commander off.
#
# 3. Cascade hits the first nonland card costing LESS. Lands are skipped
#    entirely, so a 37-land deck does not dilute it. Every card under seven mana
#    is therefore a cascade hit, which is why there is no filler: the worst
#    thing you can flip is a mana rock, and this deck is happy to flip a rock.
#
# 4. Density is the deckbuilding constraint. Thirty of the sixty-three nonland
#    cards cost seven or more. That is an absurd number by normal standards and
#    the correct one here — they are the engine, not the top end.
#
# 5. Colorless means {C}, not "generic". Zhulodok, Thought-Knot Seer, Reality
#    Smasher, Endbringer and Devourer of Destiny all want true colorless mana.
#    Every land in the deck produces it, which is the upside of giving up
#    colour entirely.
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# THE CARDS THAT CARRY IT
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
# S tier — the deck is a pile of expensive cards without these
#
#   Eye of Ugin ........... every colorless Eldrazi costs {2} less, forever,
#                           from a land slot. Mana value is untouched, so the
#                           cascade still fires. Also tutors for {7}.
#   Conduit of Ruin ....... tutors a 7+ Eldrazi to the TOP of your library and
#                           makes the first creature each turn {2} cheaper.
#                           Finds Zhulodok, then discounts him.
#   Sol Ring / Thran Dynamo / Gilded Lotus
#                           the difference between Zhulodok on four and
#                           Zhulodok on seven.
#   Ancient Tomb .......... two mana from one land. The only Game Changer in
#                           the deck and the reason it reads bracket 3.
#   Sanctum of Ugin ....... sacrifice it on any 7+ cast to tutor the next
#                           fatty. This is what turns one big turn into two.
#   All Is Dust ........... seven mana, so it cascades twice, and it wipes
#                           every coloured permanent while your board — which
#                           is entirely colorless — stands untouched.
#
# A tier — the payoffs
#
#   Ulamog, the Ceaseless Hunger
#                           exiles two permanents on CAST, so it does its job
#                           even countered. Indestructible, mills twenty a hit.
#   It That Betrays ....... annihilator 2, and everything they sacrifice comes
#                           back under your control. Wins the game by itself.
#   Emrakul, the Promised End
#                           costs {1} less per card type in your graveyard, and
#                           you play their turn for them.
#   Emrakul, the World Anew
#                           take every creature your target controls. Protection
#                           from spells means almost nothing answers it.
#   Ulamog's Dreadsire .... {T}: make a 10/10. Every turn. Forever.
#   Void Winnower ......... opponents cannot cast even-cost spells or block
#                           with even-cost creatures. Against the wrong deck
#                           this is a lock.
#   Ugin, the Spirit Dragon
#                           eight mana one-sided wrath. Exiles every coloured
#                           permanent; your entire board survives.
#   Deceiver of Form ...... reveal the top card each combat and turn the rest
#                           of your creatures into copies of it. In a deck of
#                           10/10s that ends games out of nowhere.
#   Walker of the Wastes .. +1/+1 for every land named Wastes, and fifteen of
#                           them are in here.
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT HERE
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
#   Processor Eldrazi ..... Blight Herder, Ruin Processor and Ulamog's
#                           Despoiler all want cards in opponents' exile. This
#                           deck has no ingest, so they are vanilla bodies
#                           wearing a synergy costume.
#   Reanimation / cheating spells into play
#                           every one of them turns the commander off. See
#                           rule 2.
#   Emrakul, the Aeons Torn and Mana Crypt
#                           both banned in Commander. Not a judgement call.
#   Mishra's Workshop ..... a second Game Changer, and one Ancient Tomb is
#                           already the whole bracket-3 budget.
#   Ulamog's Crusher, Pathrazer of Ulamog
#                           cut on purpose. Their entire contribution was
#                           annihilator, and the brief asked for that softened.
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# HOW IT PLAYS
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
#   Turns 1-3   Rocks. Sol Ring, Mind Stone, Worn Powerstone, Wayfarer's
#               Bauble. Thought-Knot Seer on four takes their best card.
#   Turn 4-5    Zhulodok. From here every seven-drop is three spells.
#   Turn 6+     Lead with the CHEAPEST 7+ spell you can cast, not the biggest.
#               Three sevens cast across two turns is six cascade triggers;
#               one thirteen-drop is two. Sandstone Oracle and Scour from
#               Existence are the ideal openers — they cost seven, do real
#               work, and pay for themselves twice.
#   Closing     Annihilator and Bane of Bala Ged strip their board while
#               twenty power of Eldrazi finishes. Rogue's Passage or Eldrazi
#               Conscription for the last swing.
#
#   The weakness: counterspells and graveyard-free exile removal on Zhulodok.
#   He is a 6/6 for six with no protection, and the deck is a lot of expensive
#   cards without him. Not of This World is in here for exactly that reason.
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# THE MANABASE
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
#   37 lands, which is high, and correct at an average mana value of 6.05.
#   The Locus package (Cloudpost, Glimmerpost, Vesuva, Thespian's Stage) and
#   the Eldrazi lands (Eye of Ugin, Eldrazi Temple, Shrine of the Forsaken
#   Gods, Ugin's Labyrinth) are why 37 lands produce far more than 37 mana.
#   Scorched Ruins eats two lands for four mana — only play it when you are
#   already flooded or already committed.
#
#   Temple of the False God is pinned to the Scourge original (SCG 143). Its
#   default printing is a Secret Lair, and the alternative is a Star Trek
#   crossover.
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# UPGRADES
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
#   More speed      Mishra's Workshop, Mana Vault. Both push past bracket 3.
#   More Eldrazi    Ulamog, the Defiler; Emrakul, the Exigent Doom; Flayer of
#                   Loyalties — all excellent, all annihilator, all re-open
#                   the question the brief settled.
#   More resilience Lightning Greaves and Swiftfoot Boots on Zhulodok.
#   Bracket 2       cut Ancient Tomb. That one card is the whole difference.
#
