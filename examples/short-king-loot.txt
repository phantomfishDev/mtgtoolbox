# ============================================================================
# SHORT KING LOOT — mono-black voltron on Loot, the Anomaly
# ============================================================================
#
# Loot, the Anomaly  {2}{B}  Legendary Creature — Beast Horror  -2/4
#
#   If Loot's power is negative, he assigns combat damage as though his
#   power were positive.
#
#   Threshold — Sacrifice another creature or planeswalker: Loot gets -2/-0
#   until end of turn. Activate only if there are seven or more cards in
#   your graveyard.
#
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 1. THE RULES THAT DECIDE EVERY CARD CHOICE
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
# 1. The loophole: every power-reduction effect in Magic is a pump spell.
#    Loot at -12/4 assigns twelve combat damage. Thirty years of black
#    "shrink a creature" cards — a design space nobody has ever wanted — are
#    suddenly the best cards available to you. Loot is the ONLY card in Magic
#    whose oracle text contains the phrase "power is negative." The deck
#    cannot be built with anyone else.
#
# 2. -X/-0 is the pump. -X/-X is removal, and never points at Loot. He has
#    four toughness, and a creature with zero toughness dies as a state-based
#    action — which means INDESTRUCTIBLE DOES NOT SAVE HIM. Darksteel Plate
#    is a trap here. This one distinction decides roughly twenty slots:
#    Torment (-3/-0) is a build-around, Clinging Darkness (-4/-1) is
#    excellent, Dismember (-5/-5) is a removal spell you point at the table.
#
# 3. -X/+X is the best line of text in the format for him, and there is
#    exactly one card with it. Belbe's Armor — "{X}, {T}: Target creature
#    gets -X/+X until end of turn" — scales with your mana AND raises his
#    toughness out of burn and sweeper range. It is the best card in the deck
#    and the reason the big-mana package is here.
#
# 4. Skulk is unconditional evasion on him. "Can't be blocked by creatures
#    with greater power" means nothing above power -2 may block, which is
#    every creature anyone will ever cast. Skeleton Key grants it, The Ring's
#    first level grants it, and Thieves' Tools reads "can't be blocked as
#    long as its power is 3 or less" — permanently true.
#
# 5. Grant hexproof, never shroud. Whispersilk Cloak and Lightning Greaves
#    would stop YOU targeting Loot with Belbe's Armor and all seven auras.
#    Swiftfoot Boots and My Precious grant hexproof instead; both are in.
#
# 6. Meekstone is one-sided. "Creatures with power 3 or greater don't untap."
#    Loot never qualifies. Your only attacker keeps untapping while the
#    table's fatties stay down — and since Loot has four toughness and cannot
#    profitably block, a card that keeps attackers tapped is defence.
#
# 7. Infect skips two thirds of the work. Loot's damage becomes poison, and
#    ten poison kills a player. Twenty-one commander damage needs him at -21
#    or three connections; Phyresis needs him at -10 once. One copy, so it is
#    reach rather than a plan.
#
# 8. Threshold wants seven cards in the graveyard, so self-mill is free
#    value. Darkblast's dredge, Ichor Slick's cycling, Stinkweed Imp,
#    Skeleton Key's loot trigger and The Ring's second level all switch on
#    Loot's own -2/-0 ability as a side effect of doing their jobs.
#
# 9. Loot is legendary, which makes One Ring to Rule Them All a one-sided
#    wrath — chapter II destroys all NONLEGENDARY creatures. In a deck that
#    needs one creature, that is a Plague Wind.
#
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 2. THE CARDS THAT CARRY THE DECK
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
# S tier — the deck does not work without these
#
#   Belbe's Armor ......... the only -X/+X in Magic. {X},{T} turns your whole
#                           mana pool into damage and raises his toughness.
#   Thieves' Tools ........ "can't be blocked as long as its power is 3 or
#                           less." On Loot that can never be false.
#   Phyresis .............. turns a -10 swing into a dead player.
#   Clinging Darkness ..... -4/-1, the best rate on a two-mana aura in black.
#   Torment ............... -3/-0. No toughness cost at all.
#   Skeleton Key .......... skulk for one mana, plus a loot trigger each hit
#                           that fills the graveyard for Threshold.
#   Call of the Ring ...... tempts you EVERY upkeep. Level 1 is skulk, level 4
#                           drains 3 per opponent per hit, and it draws a card
#                           each time. Free evasion, nothing on the board.
#   Cabal Coffers+Urborg .. Belbe's Armor's X. Urborg makes every land a
#                           Swamp, so Coffers and Crypt Ghast both go vertical.
#   Meekstone ............. one-sided stax for a commander never at power 3+.
#   My Precious ........... hexproof AND can't be blocked, on one card.
#
# A tier — the engines and the resilience
#
#   Agatha's Soul Cauldron  exile a Lemure, get a +1/+1 counter on Loot, and
#                           he now has "{0}: gets -1/-0 and gains flying."
#                           Unlimited and free. One tap sets up both halves.
#   Hyalopterous Lemure ... the Cauldron target. The {0} is on ITSELF on the
#                           battlefield, which is useless — its whole value is
#                           being in the graveyard.
#   Garruk, Veiled Butcher  +2 for -4/-1 that lasts UNTIL YOUR NEXT TURN, so
#                           it is live during your combat. Free every turn,
#                           and legal fodder for Loot's own Threshold ability.
#   Greel's Caress ........ -3/-0 with flash. Ambush blocker math.
#   Shirei ................ returns everything with power 1 or less at the
#                           next end step. Cabal Trainee, Reassembling
#                           Skeleton and Ophiomancer's Snakes all recur.
#   Cabal Trainee ......... sac for -2/-0. With Shirei that is every turn.
#   Ghosts of the Damned .. {T}: -1/-0, forever, for nothing. A 0/2 nobody
#                           bothers to kill.
#   Morgul-Knife Wound .... -3/-0 for two. The 2 life per upkeep is cheap at
#                           that rate.
#   Reassembling Skeleton . the mana sink. {1}{B} to return, sac to Loot for
#                           -2/-0, repeat as deep as your mana goes.
#   Stronghold Overseer ... {B}{B}: creatures without shadow get -1/-0 —
#                           activate as often as you can pay, and Loot has no
#                           shadow. Also a 5/5 flier that cannot be blocked.
#   Slagwurm Armor ........ +0/+6 for one mana. Takes Loot out of range of
#                           Languish, Blasphemous Act and every -X/-X spell.
#   Swiftfoot Boots ....... hexproof and haste. Hexproof, not shroud.
#   One Ring to Rule ...... chapter II is a wrath that spares your commander.
#   Proft ................. {B}, discard him from hand: -3/-1. A pump spell
#                           that costs no board slot and fuels Threshold.
#   Crypt Ghast /
#   Nirkana Revenant ...... double your black. Both exist to make Belbe's
#                           Armor lethal in one activation.
#
# B tier — good, swappable
#
#   Despondency ........... -2/-0, and it returns to hand from the graveyard,
#                           so removal on Loot does not eat it.
#   Mourning .............. -2/-0, and {B} returns it to hand — pick it up in
#                           response to removal.
#   Ophiomancer ........... a 1/1 deathtouch Snake every upkeep: free
#                           Threshold fodder and a blocker Loot badly wants.
#   Qarsi Revenant ........ renew puts flying, deathtouch and lifelink
#                           COUNTERS on Loot. Removal cannot strip those.
#   Key to the Side-Door .. {2},{T}: unblockable, repeatable, one mana.
#   Endling ............... through the Cauldron it hands Loot menace,
#                           deathtouch AND undying.
#   Trigon of Corruption .. three -1/-1 counters, rechargeable with {B}{B}.
#   Sinister Gnarlbark .... a card every end step, and blight 1 can go on a
#                           Snake when you do not want it on Loot.
#   Marsh Gas ............. -2/-0 to all creatures for {B} at instant speed.
#                           Shrinks blockers and pumps Loot in one breath.
#   Cursed Flesh .......... -1/-1 AND fear for one mana. The only card here
#                           that is a pump spell and an evasion source at once.
#   Basilisk Collar ....... deathtouch and lifelink for one. Mostly a "please
#                           do not block" sign.
#   Phyrexian Defiler ..... -3/-3 at instant speed, or three more damage.
#   Death's-Head Buzzard .. a flier that sweeps x/1s when it dies, and it dies
#                           to Loot's own ability.
#   Viscid Lemures ........ redundancy for the Cauldron line.
#   Inherited Envelope .... mana plus a Ring tempt, which is the skulk.
#
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 3. TRAPS — popular or obvious here, and wrong
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
#   Transmutation ........ switches power and toughness. Loot becomes 4/-2
#                          and dies on the spot. There is no state in this
#                          deck where it helps — his power is negative by
#                          design, so switching always puts the negative
#                          number on toughness.
#   Depressurize ......... -3/-0, "then if that creature's power is 0 or less,
#                          destroy it." Reads like the pump spell you want and
#                          kills him instead.
#   Multiply by Zero ..... base 0/0 until end of turn. Dies. (Good removal.)
#   Darksteel Plate ...... indestructible does NOT stop a creature with 0
#                          toughness dying — that is a state-based action, not
#                          destruction. Useless against how Loot actually dies.
#   Whispersilk Cloak,
#   Lightning Greaves .... shroud. Stops you targeting your own commander with
#                          Belbe's Armor and all seven auras.
#   Behind the Scenes .... skulk for the whole team and perfect on paper, but
#                          its {4}{W} ability makes the colour identity W/B.
#                          ILLEGAL under a mono-black commander.
#   Grafted Exoskeleton .. +2/+2 is two damage OFF, and it sacrifices Loot
#                          when it becomes unattached. Phyresis is clean.
#   Minas Morgul ......... the shadow counter grants the WRAITH TYPE, not
#                          shadow. The reminder text misleads; no evasion.
#   Exotic Curse,
#   Drag Down ............ domain, and this deck has one basic land type.
#   Pith Driller ......... five mana for one -1/-1 counter, wrong axis.
#   Languish,
#   Night of Souls'
#   Betrayal ............. kill Loot through toughness.
#   Liliana of the Dark
#   Realms ............... her -3 is -X/-X, so the "pump" mode kills him
#                          unless his toughness is already padded.
#   Azula Always Lies .... cut on the art rule — Avatar: The Last Airbender
#                          does not sit with the LOTR and Phyrexian frames.
#   Mechanical Glider .... one-mana flying and you own it, but it exists only
#                          in the Avatar set, same problem as Azula. Cursed
#                          Flesh took the slot. Swap it back if you prefer.
#   Malakir Rebirth ...... cut at your request. Feign Death took the slot:
#                          same {B} save, and the +1/+1 counter it leaves
#                          switches on Agatha's Soul Cauldron, which Malakir
#                          Rebirth did not. The cost is the land on its back,
#                          so this is 34 lands rather than an effective 35 —
#                          turn Feign Death into a 21st Swamp if the mana ever
#                          feels short.
#
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 4. MANA CURVE — 66 spells, 34 lands, average mana value 2.50
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
#    1  ###############  15
#    2  #######################  23
#    3  ################  16
#    4  ######  6
#    5  ####  4
#    6  ##  2
#   7+  0
#
# The shape is deliberate. Voltron wants to cast the commander plus two things
# in the same turn from turn four onward, because the clock is "Loot connects
# three times" and each connection needs a new pump or a new piece of evasion.
# Thirty-eight cards at one or two mana is what lets you redeploy after a
# removal spell in a single turn instead of two.
#
# Where the printed curve lies to you:
#   - Belbe's Armor is an X-cost engine, not a three-drop. Its real cost is
#     however much mana you have, frequently ten or more with Coffers.
#   - Reassembling Skeleton is a repeatable {1}{B}, the deck's mana sink.
#   - Hyalopterous Lemure and Viscid Lemures cost nothing in practice — you
#     want them in the graveyard, so the five-mana price is irrelevant.
#   - Proft is a one-mana instant most games; you discard him, not cast him.
#   - Jet Medallion is the one cost reducer, and 23 cards sit at MV 2.
#   - Feign Death is a one-mana Cauldron enabler as well as a save: the +1/+1
#     counter is exactly what the Cauldron needs to switch on the Lemure.
#
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 5. GAME PLAN
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
# Turns 1-3 — build mana, hold Loot. Sol Ring, a Signet, Mind Stone. Do NOT
# jam Loot into an open board on turn three unless you can protect him or you
# are happy to recast; he is a -2/4 and the table will point removal at him.
# Cabal Trainee, Skeleton Key, Meekstone and Slagwurm Armor are all one mana.
#
# Turn 3-4 — Loot plus a pump, and start the clock. The ideal first attack is
# Loot + Thieves' Tools (unblockable) + Clinging Darkness: a -6/3 connecting
# for six, a quarter of a commander-damage kill on turn four.
#
# Turns 5-7 — the engine turns, in rough order of preference:
#
#   1. Belbe's Armor with Coffers mana. Tap for -8/+8 or more; Loot is -10/12,
#      swings for ten, and is out of every sweeper's range. With Phyresis that
#      is a dead player on the spot.
#   2. The Cauldron line. Tap Agatha's Soul Cauldron exiling Hyalopterous
#      Lemure from your graveyard, putting a +1/+1 counter on Loot. He now has
#      "{0}: gets -1/-0 and gains flying." No cost, no limit. Announce the
#      number and attack in the air.
#   3. Aura stacking. Torment, Greel's Caress and Morgul-Knife Wound put him
#      at -11/4 for six mana across two turns.
#   4. The grind. Shirei + Cabal Trainee is -2/-0 every turn forever, Garruk's
#      +2 is another -4/-1 every turn, Ghosts of the Damned taps for -1/-0.
#
# Sequencing that is not obvious:
#   - Evasion before pump. A -14/4 Loot chump-blocked by a 1/1 did nothing.
#   - Belbe's Armor and Greel's Caress go AFTER blockers are declared. The
#     Armor is an activated ability and Greel's Caress has flash; holding them
#     hides the damage and dodges a response.
#   - Loot's Threshold ability is instant speed and costs no mana. Count your
#     graveyard before combat, then sac Snakes and Skeletons after blockers.
#   - Hold one protection spell. Feign Death, Not Dead After All and
#     Supernatural Stamina for {B} are the difference between a lost commander
#     and a lost turn. Hold Feign Death if the Cauldron is out — Loot comes
#     back with the +1/+1 counter it needs.
#   - Mourning can be picked up. {B} returns it to hand in response to removal
#     aimed at Loot, so the aura survives even when he does not.
#
# Main weakness: it is one creature. Loot is a three-mana legend with four
# toughness in a format full of edicts, Swords to Plowshares and -X/-X. The
# answers are Swiftfoot Boots, My Precious, Slagwurm Armor, Feign Death, Not
# Dead After All and Supernatural Stamina — six cards, and expect to need all
# of them. Recasting Loot costs three mana, which is the real reason the curve
# is this low.
#
# Second weakness: enchantment removal aimed at you. Pacifism, Imprisoned in
# the Moon and Darksteel Mutation shut the deck off cold and black cannot
# answer most of them. FEED THE SWARM is the only card here that kills an
# enchantment. Do not spend it on something else.
#
# Threat assessment: Loot looks absurd and reads as a joke until he hits once,
# so you usually get a free turn or two. Spend them. Once someone has seen a
# -12 swing you are archenemy, and Meekstone plus Snakes plus the -X/-X
# removal is the whole defence. Win from 40 life, not from 15.
#
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 6. MANABASE — 34 lands
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
# Two below a normal mono-black count, deliberately. The curve tops out at six
# with two cards there, average MV is 2.50, and seven nonlands are mana
# sources. What this deck cannot afford is a flooded hand when the plan is
# "recast a three-mana commander and equip something."
#
#   Big mana ........ Cabal Coffers, Urborg, Nykthos, Myriad Landscape
#   Evasion ......... Rogue's Passage, Shizo
#   Recursion ....... Volrath's Stronghold, Mortuary Mire, Witch's Cottage,
#                     Takenuma
#   Card advantage .. Castle Locthwain
#   Sac outlet ...... Phyrexian Tower
#   Utility ......... Bojuka Bog, Dragon-Cursed Halls
#   Basics .......... 20 Swamp
#
# Black sources: 32 of 34 (all but Rogue's Passage and Dragon-Cursed Halls),
# plus Charcoal Diamond, Arcane Signet, Jet Medallion and Inherited Envelope.
#
# Urborg does more than it looks like: it makes all 34 lands Swamps, which
# turns Cabal Coffers into a real engine, makes Crypt Ghast trigger off every
# land, and switches on Shizo and Witch's Cottage whatever else you drew.
#
# Lands to skip: Minas Morgul (Wraith type, not shadow), Crypt of Agadeem
# (enters tapped, wants a wide creature graveyard this deck lacks), Blast Zone
# (kills Loot). Dragon-Cursed Halls is a real cost and is in because you own
# it and the Marta Nael art fits — a colourless-only land in a Cabal Coffers
# deck is the weakest land here, and the first Swamp swap if mana feels short.
#
#
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
# 7. BRACKET 3, AND UPGRADE PATHS
# ---------------------------------------------------------------------------
#
# `mtgproxy bracket` assesses this as bracket 2 on contents: zero Game
# Changers, no mass land denial, no extra turns. Call it a 3 at the table
# anyway, because of one thing the tool explicitly does not check — Agatha's
# Soul Cauldron plus a Lemure in the graveyard produces unlimited free power
# reduction, and with Phyresis that one-shots a player from an empty board.
# It is not infinite (no mana, no cards) but it ends someone.
#
# Honest table pitch: "mono-black voltron, no Game Changers, there's one
# two-card line that can one-shot someone through poison."
#
# To play it as a true bracket 2: cut Agatha's Soul Cauldron, Hyalopterous
# Lemure, Viscid Lemures and Phyresis for Bone Flute, Staff of Zegon,
# Feebleness and Serrated Arrows. Slower, wins by plain commander damage.
#
# Upgrades, by goal:
#   speed ....... Buried Alive finds both Lemures and puts them straight in
#                 the graveyard, turning the Cauldron line into a reliable
#                 turn-five kill with Phyresis. Entomb does it for one mana.
#                 Both were left out on purpose — you asked for the combo as a
#                 natural draw. This is the single biggest power jump here.
#   consistency . Tainted Strike is a second infect outlet for {B}. Staff of
#                 Zegon and Bone Flute are two more repeatable -X/-0 engines
#                 if the pump ever feels thin.
#   resilience .. Whip of Erebos, Undying Malice (a functional reprint of
#                 Feign Death), Imp's Mischief to redirect the removal that
#                 matters. Vampiric Link turns a -12 connection into 12 life,
#                 which answers "win from 40, not 15" directly.
#   grind ....... Zulaport Cutthroat and Bastion of Remembrance alongside
#                 Blood Artist make the sacrifice engine a second win
#                 condition that never needs Loot to connect.
#
# Cards you listed that did not make it:
#   Pith Driller ........... five mana, one -1/-1 counter, wrong axis.
#   Exotic Curse, Drag Down  domain, one basic land type.
#   Afflict ................ -1/-1 and a card is fine, 66th-best here.
#   Toxin Analysis ......... Basilisk Collar does deathtouch permanently, and
#                            deathtouch is near-dead on an unblockable body.
#   Feebleness ............. -2/-1 is worse than Despondency/Mourning at -2/-0.
#   Gideon the Oathless,
#   Sanctum Lurker,
#   Cast Away Doubt,
#   Extended Absence ....... no planeswalker synergy, and Cast Away Doubt's
#                            symmetric damage points the wrong way in a deck
#                            that wants to win at high life.
#   Gollum Patient Plotter,
#   Sam's Desperate Rescue,
#   Gollum's Bite .......... all fine, all 100th-best. Gollum is the first
#                            card back in if you want more Ring tempts.
#
#
